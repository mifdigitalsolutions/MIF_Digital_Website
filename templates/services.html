Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, pricing, payments and ongoing support.

Can I request a custom package?

Absolutely. MIF Digital Solutions offers customised solutions based on your business goals, required features, budget and timeline. Contact our team to discuss your requirements and receive a tailored proposal.

Can I pay online?

Yes. You can select your preferred service package, provide your email address and proceed to our payment checkout. Payment confirmation will be provided after a successful transaction. Online payments are subject to live payment gateway availability.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration and hosting are not automatically included in our service packages. They can be arranged as part of your project or billed separately. All costs will be clearly stated in your quotation before work begins.

How do I get support after delivery?

MIF Digital Solutions provides post-delivery support according to the support or maintenance plan agreed upon for your project. Contact us through our website to request assistance, report an issue or discuss additional improvements.
